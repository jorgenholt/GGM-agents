The tests are designed so that the master's thesis can run them on the 2023 data without new expert work.

A further test applies to the system as a whole: whether it needs fewer corrections the longer it is used.
Recording every accepted, edited and rejected suggestion makes this measurable as the share of suggestions accepted unchanged over successive working sessions.

This answers the third research question.
Agents could take over most of the reading, extraction and mapping in the data work, and support the diagnosis in calibration and the explanation of results.
Ordinary code should do every computation and every model run.
The analyst should remain responsible for the question, the storyline and its projections, the acceptance of the calibration, and the interpretation, and the system is designed so that the analyst's corrections improve it over time.
Chapter 6 discusses the risks of this division and which agent to build first.
