\section{Research question}

% TODO: Provisional wording, revised 2026-10-15. To be agreed with Franziska and Lukas.

The thesis asks:

\begin{quote}
Where in the workflow around the Global Gas Model could LLM-based agents take over or support expert work, where would ordinary code serve better, and where should the work stay with a person?
\end{quote}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What does the workflow around GGM consist of, and where do the effort and judgment in it lie? (Chapter 3)
  \item What kinds of tasks are LLM agents suited to, and which are better done by ordinary code? (Chapter 4)
  \item For each step of the workflow, what could an agent do, and where must a person remain involved? (Chapter 5)
  \item What would it take for an agent to support the calibration of a model whose parameters carry economic judgment? (Chapters 5 and 6)
\end{enumerate}
