\section{Research question}

The thesis addresses one main question:

\begin{quote}
Where in the workflow around the Global Gas Model could LLM-based agents take over or support expert work, where would ordinary code serve better, and where should the work stay with a person?
\end{quote}

It is broken into three sub-questions, each answered mainly in one chapter:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What does the workflow around GGM consist of, and where in it do the expert effort and judgment lie? (Chapter 3)
  \item What kinds of tasks are LLM agents suited to, and which are better done by ordinary code? (Chapter 4)
  \item For each step of the GGM workflow, what could an agent do, and where must a person remain involved? (Chapter 5)
\end{enumerate}

The answers are brought together in Chapter 6 as a sketch of the system the master's thesis would build, and Chapter 7 discusses what the analysis leaves unsettled.
